How our staffing works
## From role brief to a placed engineer — without the back‑and‑forth.
A repeatable, transparent process. You see exactly where each requisition stands at every stage, and you only ever review candidates we'd hire ourselves.
- 01
- Intake & role brief
- We map the role, must‑have skills, team context and budget so the search starts precise.
- 02
- Source & screen
- Our recruiters surface candidates from our active network and screen for fit, intent and availability.
- 03
- Technical vetting
- Each candidate is assessed by domain specialists — code, architecture and real‑world scenario questions.
- 04
- Submit shortlist
- You receive a tight, ranked shortlist with notes — after the brief is aligned.
- 05
- Place & support
- We handle offers, onboarding, payroll and check‑ins so the placement holds for the long run.

Staffing FAQ
## Answers before you ask.
How fast can you submit candidates?
For every role, we start with an aligned brief, then communicate clearly about candidate fit, timing and search complexity.
What engagement types do you offer?
Contract, contract‑to‑hire and direct placement — whichever fits the role and budget. Many clients start contract to de‑risk, then convert strong performers to full‑time later.
How do you support onboarding?
Yes. We coordinate onboarding, compliance steps and candidate communication so your team can keep the process moving cleanly.
What if a placement doesn't work out?
Every placement carries a replacement guarantee period. If the fit isn't right in those early weeks, we re‑open the search and backfill at no extra placement fee — the risk stays with us, not you.
