Overview
## Infrastructure that stays up.
Most estates grow by accretion. Servers are added when a project needs them, a cloud account spins up here, a legacy box lingers there — and a few years on the result is fragile, expensive and hard to secure. When that foundation wobbles, the cost lands everywhere at once: stalled releases, frustrated users, an outage that drags a whole business day with it.
We take a calmer approach. Zangle designs, builds and operates infrastructure that is resilient by default and secure from the ground up — across cloud, hybrid and on‑premises footprints. We map what you already run, retire what no longer earns its keep, and put monitoring, backup and recovery in place before they are ever needed, so reliability becomes the baseline rather than the exception.

What we deliver
## Every layer of a dependable estate.
From the network underneath to the recovery plan above, we cover the full stack — and treat each layer as part of one system rather than a set of disconnected boxes.
Cloud & hybrid infrastructure
We architect workloads where they belong — public cloud, private data centre or a deliberate mix — with landing zones, networking and governance set up so the environment scales without sprawl or surprise bills.
Networking & connectivity
Segmented networks, secure site‑to‑site and remote access, SD‑WAN and DNS designed for performance and isolation — so traffic moves fast, stays contained, and a single subnet never becomes a single point of failure.
Virtualization & compute
Hypervisor platforms, container hosts and right‑sized compute that consolidate workloads, lift utilisation and give you headroom to grow — provisioned as code so a rebuild is hours, not days.
Identity & security
Directory services, single sign‑on, multi‑factor and least‑privilege access, with hardening, patching and segmentation woven through the build. Security is a property of the design, not a tool you buy afterwards.
Monitoring & observability
Metrics, logs and tracing wired into clear dashboards and meaningful alerts, so issues surface as early warnings — not as 2 a.m. outages — and your team always knows what the estate is doing.
Backup & disaster recovery
Tested backups, defined recovery objectives and runbooks you can actually follow under pressure. We rehearse the failure so that, when something does go wrong, recovery is a routine you've already practised.

FAQ
## Infrastructure FAQ.
### Cloud, on‑premises or hybrid — what do you recommend?
It depends on your workloads, data, compliance needs and cost profile — there is no single right answer. We assess each system on its merits: some belong in public cloud for elasticity, others stay on‑premises for latency, control or economics. Most clients land on a deliberate hybrid, and we make those trade‑offs explicit rather than assumed.
### How do you secure our infrastructure?
Security is part of the design, not a later add‑on. We build on strong identity and least‑privilege access, multi‑factor authentication, network segmentation, disciplined patching and hardened baselines, then validate the result. The aim is a smaller attack surface and clear visibility — so a misconfiguration is caught in review, not in an incident.
### Do you handle disaster recovery and backups?
Yes — and we treat them as first‑class deliverables. We define recovery time and recovery point objectives with you, implement protected, regularly tested backups, and document failover runbooks your team can follow under pressure. A backup that has never been restored isn't a recovery plan, so we rehearse it.
### Can you provide managed operations?
Yes. We shape support around the requirement, from business-hours coordination to critical-incident response, with staffing support aligned to the skills your environment needs.
