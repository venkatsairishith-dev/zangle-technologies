How we work
## A culture built on four simple values.
The same principles guide every leader and every team — from a first staffing conversation to a production support call.
Accountability
We name an owner for every commitment and stand behind the result — no hand-offs into the gaps.
Rigor
Decisions are grounded in evidence and review, not opinion — we measure, then we move.
People first
We invest in the careers of our people and the success of our clients — both have to win.
Momentum
We ship in small, steady increments and keep work visible, so progress never stalls in silence.
