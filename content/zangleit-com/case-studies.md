Engagement approach
## A cleaner way to move from requirement to qualified talent.
01
### Requirement discovery
We clarify the business problem, technical environment, role expectations and hiring timeline before sourcing begins.
02
### Skill alignment
Candidates are reviewed against the tools, standards, platforms, frameworks and team context that matter for the role.
03
### Shortlist clarity
Each introduction is framed with concise context so hiring teams understand why a candidate fits the need.
04
### Ongoing communication
We keep feedback loops direct, practical and transparent so the search improves as the team learns.
How we measure success
## The numbers are agreed before the work begins.
Outcome‑driven scope
Every engagement opens with the metric that defines success — time‑to‑submit, fit, timing, schedule or feedback — written into the scope.
Shared dashboards
Progress is visible in real time. Clients see the same dashboards we do — no surprises, no quarter‑end reveals.
Accountable delivery
We own the result, not just the activity — measured against the targets we agreed, and answerable for the gap if there is one.
