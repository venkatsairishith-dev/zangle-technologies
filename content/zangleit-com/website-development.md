FAQ
## Website FAQ
### WordPress or a custom build — which is right for us?
It depends on how you work. If your team publishes content often and wants independence, a well‑structured WordPress or Webflow site is usually the right call. If you need bespoke functionality, complex data or app‑like interactions, a custom React/Next.js build gives more control. We recommend based on your goals, budget and who maintains the site — not a default.
### Do you handle SEO?
Yes. Technical SEO is part of every build: clean semantic markup, fast pages, structured data, sensible metadata, sitemaps and crawlable architecture. We also set up analytics and search tooling so you can track rankings and traffic. For ongoing content and link strategy we can advise or work alongside your marketing team.
### Can you maintain the site after launch?
Absolutely. We offer ongoing maintenance and support covering updates, security patches, backups, performance monitoring and small enhancements. Plans scale from light, on‑demand support to a regular roadmap of improvements, so your site keeps performing well after go‑live.
### Do you build e‑commerce?
Yes — from Shopify storefronts to headless commerce on a custom front‑end. We optimise product and category pages, streamline checkout, and integrate payment, shipping, tax and inventory systems. The aim is a store that converts well, loads fast and is straightforward for your team to manage day to day.
