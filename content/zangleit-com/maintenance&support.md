Overview
## Keep it running. Keep it improving.
Software does not stay healthy on its own. Left unattended, platforms accumulate the quiet costs of neglect — unplanned downtime that erodes trust, fixes that arrive slowly because nobody owns the system, and a steady drift away from current security baselines as patches pile up. Each of these compounds, and by the time they surface as an incident, the cheapest moment to act has already passed.
Our approach is to get ahead of failure rather than chase it. We instrument what matters and watch it continuously, structure support into clear tiers so the right person handles the right problem, and commit to explicit service levels so you always know what response to expect. Maintenance is treated as an ongoing program of improvement, not a queue of tickets — small, deliberate gains accumulating into a system that is more reliable every quarter.

What we cover
## Everything it takes to keep production steady.
proactive monitoring & alerting
Health checks, synthetic probes and threshold alerts across application, database and infrastructure layers, tuned to cut noise and surface real signals before users feel them.
Incident response
A defined runbook for triage, escalation and communication, with severity-based SLAs so the right engineers are engaged fast and stakeholders stay informed until resolution.
Patching & updates
Scheduled OS, runtime and dependency patching with vulnerability tracking, tested in staging and rolled out through controlled windows to close security gaps without surprises.
Application support
Day-to-day care of your applications — bug fixes, small enhancements, configuration changes and user-reported issues handled by engineers who know your codebase.
Performance tuning
Profiling slow paths, optimising queries and caching, and right-sizing resources so the platform stays fast and cost-efficient as traffic and data grow.
Backup & recovery
Automated, verified backups with documented restore procedures and periodic recovery drills, so a failure or data loss event has a tested path back to normal.
Support model
## Support tiers.
L1
Triage & first response
The front line: acknowledges every issue, classifies severity, resolves known and routine requests, and escalates with full context when deeper work is needed.
L2
Engineering & fixes
Hands-on engineers who reproduce defects, debug across the stack, ship code and configuration fixes, and handle the changes that go beyond standard procedures.
L3
Deep & architectural
Senior specialists for the hardest problems — root-cause analysis of systemic faults, architectural remediation, and the structural changes that stop incidents recurring.

## Support FAQ
What SLAs do you offer?
We agree service levels up front, with response and resolution targets set by incident severity. Critical, production-down issues carry the tightest commitments; lower-priority requests follow defined but more relaxed windows. Targets are documented, measured and reported, so expectations are explicit on both sides.
How is support scoped?
Support is scoped to the environment and risk level. We can combine monitoring, response planning and routine maintenance so teams get the right coverage without unclear promises.
What systems do you support?
Web and SaaS applications, APIs and backend services, databases, and the cloud or on-premise infrastructure they run on. We work across common stacks and major cloud platforms, and during onboarding we map your specific environment so support is grounded in how your systems are actually built.
How fast do you respond to incidents?
Response time depends on severity and your SLA tier. High-severity incidents trigger immediate acknowledgement and escalation through the on-call rotation, while standard requests are picked up within agreed windows. Whatever the priority, every issue is acknowledged and tracked so nothing is lost.
