## Clinical staffing FAQ
### How do you credential candidates?
Every clinician goes through a structured workflow: primary-source license verification, background and exclusion (OIG/SAM) screening, immunization records, reference checks and a skills assessment. Documentation is compiled and ready for your audit file at submission.
### How fast can you submit?
For most roles we deliver a pre-credentialed shortlist within about 72 hours of the role brief. Hard-to-fill specialties may take longer, but we keep you updated and never trade speed for skipped verification.
### What roles do you cover?
Registered nurses and specialty nursing, allied health, clinical informatics, lab and imaging, care coordination and revenue-cycle roles — on per-diem, contract, contract-to-hire and direct-placement terms.
### Do you handle compliance and screening?
Yes. Compliance and screening are part of every placement, not an add-on. We manage background checks, exclusion screening, license and immunization verification, and keep records current for the duration of the assignment.
