Overview
## Strategy that ships.
Most transformations don't fail in the boardroom — they stall in the gap between strategy and execution. A deck sets the ambition, then ownership fragments across vendors, internal teams and quarterly reprioritization, and the costed roadmap quietly becomes shelfware. The result is spend without momentum: real budget consumed, very little of the platform actually changed.
Zangle closes that gap by advising and delivering. We own the roadmap and the build — the same accountable team that frames the business case writes the architecture, stands up the squads and ships the increments. Strategy stays honest because the people who drew it are the ones who have to make it run, and delivery stays aligned because there's no handoff to lose the intent.

## IT consulting FAQ
### Do you only advise, or do you deliver too?
Both — and that's the point. The team that frames the strategy is the team accountable for shipping it. You can engage us for advisory alone, but most clients want the same group to carry the roadmap through to running software.
### How do you measure ROI on a transformation?
We set the business case up front — the metrics, baselines and targets the investment is meant to move — then track delivery against them release by release. ROI is judged on value in production, not story points burned.
### What engagement models do you offer?
Fixed-scope advisory, time-and-materials delivery squads, and outcome-based programmes with shared milestones. We'll recommend the model that fits the risk profile and how clearly the scope is defined.
### Can you augment our existing team?
Yes. We frequently embed architects, delivery leads and engineers alongside in-house staff — adding capability and pace without displacing the people who'll own the platform long term. See our
