Our commitment
## Mission‑ready talent, civilian careers.
Veterans and military spouses bring something most résumés can't capture — discipline under pressure, real leadership, and a hard‑earned instinct for solving problems with whatever is in front of them. Those are exactly the traits that high‑performing technology teams are built on, and they translate directly into engineering, security, cloud and program roles.
Zangle is committed to recruiting, upskilling and placing those who served. We meet veterans where they are — helping translate military experience into civilian terms, supporting certifications that open doors, and standing beside them through interviews and onboarding until they're settled into a career, not just a job.

## Veteran hiring FAQ
### Do I need a tech background?
No. Many of the veterans we place start without formal IT experience. What matters is your aptitude and drive — we provide the certification pathways and mentorship to build the technical skills around the strengths you already have.
### Do you help with certifications?
Yes. We support and guide industry certifications in cloud, security and data — and help you make the most of GI Bill and employer‑funded options — so the cost of upskilling never stands between you and the role.
### Is there support for military spouses?
Absolutely. We prioritise portable, remote‑friendly roles and offer career continuity support designed for military families, so a spouse's career can move with you rather than restart with every PCS.
