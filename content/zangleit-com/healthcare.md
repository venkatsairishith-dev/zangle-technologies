Questions
## Healthcare IT FAQ.
### Do you ensure HIPAA compliance?
Yes. We architect every solution around HIPAA and HITECH safeguards — PHI encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, audit logging and documented policies — and work alongside your compliance team so the evidence is there when it's needed.
### Can you integrate with our EHR?
We work across major EHR/EMR platforms — Epic, Cerner, MEDITECH and others — using their integration interfaces, HL7 v2, FHIR APIs and secure middleware to connect records, scheduling, labs and billing without disrupting clinical operations.
### Do you support HL7/FHIR interoperability?
Yes — interoperability is core to our practice. We build standards-based exchange with HL7 v2 messaging and FHIR R4 resources and APIs, connecting EHRs, labs, imaging, devices and payers into a coherent, governed data flow.
### Can you provide clinical and IT staff?
We do. Our healthcare staffing practice places credentialed clinicians, EHR analysts, integration engineers, security specialists and support staff — for project surges, backfill or long-term roles — all vetted for healthcare-specific skills and compliance.
