FAQ
## Outsourcing FAQ
### the right delivery model — which is better for us?
It depends on the work. Nearshore gives you near real‑time overlap for collaborative, fast‑moving work; engineering maximises cost efficiency and round‑the‑clock throughput. Many clients blend both, and we help you decide based on time zones, communication needs and budget.
### How do you ensure quality and communication?
Teams work to your definition of done — your code review, testing and acceptance standards. A named delivery lead owns the relationship, you get regular stand‑ups, sprint reviews and reporting, and the team lives in your Jira, Slack and Git so progress is always visible.
### How is our IP protected?
Intellectual property remains entirely yours. Engagements are covered by NDAs and IP‑assignment clauses, access is least‑privilege and audited, and we work within your security controls — from device policy to data handling — so nothing sensitive leaves your boundary.
### How quickly can a team scale up or down?
Most teams reach productive capacity within a few weeks, and we add or release roles on agreed notice as your roadmap shifts. Because we carry the bench and recruiting pipeline, you can ramp for a launch and ease off afterwards without carrying idle headcount.
